## Plant notes

**Tree** — Thirsty, dense roots; everything under it dries out faster than it looks. Don't pile soil or mulch against the trunk (collar rot).

**Peace lily** — Pale yellow-green leaves + brown tips = rootbound / hungry / too much direct sun. Repot one size up, flush the soil, liquid feed, move somewhere shadier.

**Corner bed** — Fallen tree leaves are fine as mulch. Add a proper mulch layer before summer. Water deeply, check below the surface.

**Elephant bush cuttings** — Succulent: let the mix dry out between waterings. Yellowing on the left cutting is usually overwatering; pinch off the yellow leaves.

**Golden baby's tears trough** — Likes steady moisture and shade. Bronzing in the middle = drying out or too much sun. Keep moist, trim brown bits; it fills back in.

**String of bananas** — Succulent. Water only when dry. Let it trail over the edge of the trough.

**Spider plants** — Easy. Water when the top few cm is dry. Brown tips = dry spells or salts in tap water.

**Fuchsia** — Clear the dead leaves out of the centre (rot/fungus risk). Cut back by about a third to nodes to make it bushy. Hang it back up; keep it evenly moist and out of afternoon sun. Feed fortnightly in the growing season.

**Spotted cane begonia** — Doing well. Pinch tall canes to encourage branching; let the top of the soil dry slightly between waterings.
